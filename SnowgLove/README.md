# North-Love
A parody christmas-themed dating-sim set in the North Pole. I beg you to not take this *'visual novel'* made with Ren'Py seriously. This is fully comedic and silly.

## Characters
- A moose desguised as a reindeer
- A snowoman
- A tourist penguin (no penguins on the North Pole)
- A clumsy polar bear

## Plot
Player is an astronomer visiting the North Pole on December to see the aurora borealis. They encounter a magical christmas landscape they didn't expect.
